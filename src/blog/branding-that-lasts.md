Good branding survives trend cycles because it's built on a few durable ideas rather than a single visual trick. Here's what we keep coming back to when we kick off a new identity project.

## Start with the story, not the palette

Colors and type are the last decision, not the first. If a client can't tell us why they exist in one sentence, we're not ready to open a design file yet.

## Systems over assets

A logo is one asset. A system is a set of rules that lets a small team ship consistent work for years without us in the room.
